The problem

When one clinical AI memory is found to be wrong, can the system discover, repair, and verify everything that inherited the error without opening every patient record?

A record assistant may store an incorrect patient association, summarize it into later memory, and reuse the derived state after the original source is corrected. Deleting the visible seed leaves authentic-looking handovers, summaries, and retrieval cues active. A full reset removes the error but destroys useful memory. Central forensic reconstruction recovers accurately but requires reading patient content the investigator is not authorized to see.

The CARE loop

C
Candidate discovery
Exact lineage plus receiver-scoped latent sketches produce a ranked candidate set only.
A
Attribution
Local counterfactual replay confirms whether the candidate actually inherited the error.
R
Recompilation
Confirmed descendants are rebuilt from trusted FHIR sources, or quarantined. Never guessed.
E
Enforcement
Tombstones and a resurrection firewall stop withdrawn influence from returning.

Role separation
