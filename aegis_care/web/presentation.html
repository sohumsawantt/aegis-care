AEGIS-Care
A privacy-bounded memory recompiler for recovering poisoned clinical AI agents.
When one clinical AI memory is found to be wrong, can the system discover, repair, and verify everything that inherited the error — without opening every patient record?

Delete the seed

Removes the known bad entry. Derived summaries, handovers, and retrieval cues stay active and keep being reused.

Wipe all memory

Safe, and it destroys every legitimate thing the assistant learned. Persistence stops paying for itself.

Central investigation

Accurate, and it requires reading patient content the investigator is not authorised to see.

Why Reset Is Worse
The safe fix: wipe every memory. Baseline C. It works, and look at what it costs.
